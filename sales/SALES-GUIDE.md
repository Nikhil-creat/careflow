# CareFlow: how to sell to local multi-speciality hospitals

## 1. Be honest about readiness
The demo keeps data in the browser only. Before real patient data or a paid contract, build: Postgres or Supabase database, staff login with roles, audit log, daily backups, HTTPS, hosting in an India region, patient consent capture, and a data processing agreement. Until then, sell a PILOT on non-clinical front-desk workflows (booking, confirmations, reminders). Do not claim HIPAA, NABH or ABDM certification you do not hold. Check India's DPDP Act 2023 and current rules with a lawyer.

## 2. Positioning
"Front-desk and patient-communication automation for 20 to 150 bed hospitals." It complements their existing billing/HMS, it does not replace it.
Pains: long queues, phone-call load, no-shows, manual reminders, missed billing, scattered WhatsApp chats.

## 3. Who to target
Multi-speciality hospitals (20 to 100 beds), nursing homes, diagnostic centres, polyclinic groups in your city. Decision makers: owner-doctor, MD, administrator, front-office or operations head.

## 4. The offer
30-day paid pilot, one workflow: AI booking + WhatsApp confirmations + reminders. Track: no-show rate, calls handled, front-desk minutes saved. Convert to monthly plan if numbers improve.

## 5. Pricing (suggested, adjust to your market)
Starter Rs 4,999/mo: booking, confirmations, reminders, one location.
Growth Rs 14,999/mo: adds Telugu/Hindi/English AI assistant, billing, lab, pharmacy, analytics, campaigns.
Enterprise: custom (multi-branch, integrations, India hosting).
Setup fee Rs 15,000 to 50,000. Annual prepay discount about 15%.
Example only: 10 hospitals at Rs 10,000 average = Rs 1 lakh/month.

## 6. Finding leads
Google Maps search "multi speciality hospital near me", local IMA chapter and hospital associations, LinkedIn (administrators, ops heads), walk in with the live demo open on your phone, referrals from doctors you know.

## 7. First message (WhatsApp)
"Namaste Dr. ___, I build an AI system that books appointments, sends confirmations and reminders on WhatsApp, and cuts no-shows. Can I show you a 10 minute demo this week? Free 30-day pilot discussion after."

## 8. Ten-minute demo
1) Chatbot books a patient and shows the confirmation card. 2) Agents tab: auto mode, reminders, rebooking. 3) Beds, lab, billing. 4) Analytics and marketing. 5) Settings: their hospital name and address live. Ask: "Which of these costs your staff the most time today?"

## 9. Objections
"We have an HMS": we sit beside it and handle patient communication. "Is data safe?": India hosting, consent, roles, audit log, signed DPA. "Staff will not adopt": WhatsApp-first, one hour training, you stay on call during week 1. "Too costly": compare with one front-desk salary or the revenue of recovered no-shows.

## 10. Contract checklist
Scope, pilot duration and success metrics, price and GST, data ownership (hospital owns data), DPA, confidentiality, support hours and response times, liability cap, termination notice, your IP stays yours, white-label terms if any.

## 11. Business basics
Register (proprietorship or LLP), Udyam/MSME, GST, invoices, business bank account, professional indemnity or cyber insurance when you handle patient data.

## 12. Rollout
Week 1: setup, branding, staff training. Week 2: live with one department. Week 3: add reminders and billing. Week 4: review numbers, collect a testimonial, propose the plan. Ask each happy hospital for two referrals.
