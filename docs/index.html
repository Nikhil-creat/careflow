Questions

Is the assistant a doctor?

No. It guides you to the right department. It does not diagnose or prescribe.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 108 or 112 or go to the nearest emergency room. The assistant tells you the same.

How do I get my appointment details?

After booking you get a card with date, time, hospital, doctor and reference. Save it to your calendar or share it on WhatsApp.

Is my data safe?

This demo keeps data only in your browser. Do not enter real patient data.
