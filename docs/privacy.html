Privacy notice

Template. Have a lawyer review it before real use.

What we collect

Name, phone, age, complaint and appointment details that you enter to book care.

Why

Only to book appointments, send confirmations and reminders, and run billing for your visit.

Where it lives

In this demo, data stays in your own browser. A production deployment must store data on secured servers in India with access controls and backups.

Your choices

You can ask to view, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, by contacting the hospital helpline.

Not medical advice

The AI assistant guides you to a department. It does not diagnose. In an emergency call 108 or 112.
